About the product

AI THIEN is independently developed by Mai Hoang Thien. The project aims to provide a practical AI assistant with locally controlled processing and optional integrations with authorized AI services.

Product capabilities

  • Android mobile AI assistant.
  • Local AI inference and processing.
  • Conversation and memory management.
  • Voice interaction capabilities.
  • Optional external AI integrations, subject to provider authorization.

Ownership

AI THIEN is a privately owned, proprietary software project. Its source code and internal implementation are not publicly distributed.

Founder and developer: Mai Hoang Thien.

Sign in with ChatGPT integration

The developer intends to request authorization for Sign in with ChatGPT and, if approved and eligible, ChatGPT plan usage for AI requests within AI THIEN.

The initial intended deployment is a single-owner Android installation. No OpenAI partnership, approval or endorsement is claimed at this time.

Privacy and security

  • Authentication credentials are intended to remain under the user's control.
  • External AI services require appropriate authorization.
  • Subscription usage will not be shared, pooled, transferred or resold.
  • Credentials must not be exposed in application logs or source control.
